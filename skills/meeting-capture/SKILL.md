# Meeting Capture

Use `knowledge-capture` in meeting mode. Read [the shared meeting procedure](../knowledge-capture/references/meetings.md) and apply it to the supplied meetings, people, date range, and authorized destinations. This is a convenient entry point, not a second routing or approval workflow.

Carry the user’s existing authorization into the disposition table: compile requested people records, apply permitted document changes, route explicitly requested todos with dates, and retain absorbed reviews. Ask only for uncovered decisions or target-required confirmations. The shared procedure owns source pagination, coaching attribution, task deduplication/read-back, and archival; do not restate or alter it here.
