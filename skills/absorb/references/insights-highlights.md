# Insights & Highlights

**Insights** are meaningful interpretations, connections or explanations. **Highlights** are useful specifics: practices, mechanisms, examples, caveats, rationale, surprising details and things to try. A practice is included by this definition; it need not appear in the heading. Preserve both general guidance and the small details that make it usable.

Select what matters to the user's question or purpose. Keep source references and uncertainty with the detail; distinguish a direct statement from an interpretation or proposed application. Do not turn a suggested experiment into a commitment or treat a source's assertion as a verified outcome. Extract proportionately: no quotas, exhaustive trivial lists or forced destinations.

Use a compact subsection or table within the existing source summary or meeting review when it makes selection legible:

| Insight or highlight | Source / uncertainty | Disposition / linked row | Landing after approval |
|---|---|---|---|

Link to existing decision, document or action rows rather than repeat their text or create a task for each detail. A disposition states what happens and why: propose a named destination; retain as source context only; defer a specific decision; or decline with a reason. Proposed and deferred details are not accepted changes. The landing column stays pending until the approved change is read back; then name the actual destination and location, or the specific missing detail. A parent document's successful update does not establish that every accepted detail landed. Keep row-level execution and archival rules in the owning workflow; retaining context is not a completed absorption row.

Consider knowledge (wiki when enabled), existing documents, actions and locally declared custom classes only where relevant; do not force every route. Custom classes follow the shared distribution contract. A teaching tip or thing to try can be retained as guidance without a task, draft, publication or experiment. Actions remain explicitly attributed commitments or proposals requiring authorization; a highlight alone supplies neither.

Reuse source-summary explanations, assessment caveats and existing disposition rows. Do not add a second inventory when those already expose the useful specifics, their selection and actual coverage. Accepted details belong in the requested working material at the appropriate level: general teaching guidance and small tips or experiments can coexist, with evidence and tentative status preserved.
